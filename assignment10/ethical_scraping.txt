Question #1: 
Which sections of the website are restricted for crawling?

Answer:

The Wikipedia robots.txt file blocks crawlers from accessing various site areas, including:
- Dynamic script and API paths such as /w/, /api/, and /trap/

- Special pages and administrative pages under /wiki/Special: and its variations

- Many internal wiki pages for deletion discussions, user restrictions, arbitration, etc., like /wiki/Wikipedia:Articles_for_deletion/ and related discussion pages

- In general, Wikipedia disallows bots from crawling non-article content and back-end or sensitive pages that aren’t meant for general indexing.
